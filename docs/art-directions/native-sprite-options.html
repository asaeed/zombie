Production target: D

Use a 24 x 32 logical grid for unit art, with a fixed foot anchor and distinct directional frames. Brighter role colors and equipment silhouettes should read before surface detail in a busy nighttime battle. The existing 12 x 16 sniper pilot proves atlas playback, not the selected art resolution. Review the new animated zombie cast at D's scale.

These are original code-drawn studies, not copied sprites or downsampled 3D renders. The heading control sketches front, back, side, and mirrored views; it is not eight finished directional frames. The chosen low-poly direction can supply model and animation references. Final unit frames would be rendered, reduced, and pixel-cleaned to the 24 x 32 grid.
